# Soviet roster integration

Two Soviet profiles and two tank-regiment templates are implemented and verified (264 profiles / 258 templates overall). Both use the approved Gen1-plus-ERA_LIGHT definition: hard defense increases from 6 to 8, with the original weapons, movement and national support retained. Each has 1,143 personnel and 94 own tanks, costs 65 prestige, and uses January 1985 (turn 564) as its authored availability anchor. After Robert refreshed, agent-run Unity tests passed 13/13 Soviet roster tests and 925/925 full EditorTests, with no failures, skips or inconclusive results, including all seven asset checks. All 347 imported PNGs now have registered profile owners; all 95 Soviet PNGs are owned. The three explicit legacy regional-profile exceptions remain. Static comparison preserves all 290 prior enum values, 262 profiles and 256 templates. Helper tests pass 4/4; only two ownership rows change, 345 remain unchanged, and all IDs/status bytes remain at revision 361. Visual/build and consumer acceptance remain open.

Both retain LOW_PROFILE and NBC_PROTECTED, use Tracked movement, movement10, primary range1, spotting2, ICM1.0, Hard target and UpgradePath.TANK. ERA_LIGHT adds +2 hard defense through the existing trait catalog. No additional fire-control, gun-launched missile, amphibious or engine upgrade is introduced. Gen1 + TANK retains cost65. January 1985 is an authored game date, not a claim of the precise delivery month or a complete historical upgrade package.

Each original Soviet tank-regiment census is retained except for its own tank token: 1,143 personnel; 94 MV tanks; 45 BMP-1; 21 BTR-70; 12 BRDM-2; 4 ZSU-57; 4 Kub; 18 2S1; 12 each of 81mm mortars, 120mm mortars, ATGM and Strela. These are game-regiment censuses under design §10.7.9, not national inventories. T-55A/T-62A profiles, original formations, combat ratings, prices and availability remain unchanged. The support census is not silently modernized.

Both templates use TANK / GroundCombat, Side.Player, Nationality.USSR, Trained experience, Secondary/Small and the new profile in Deployed. Mobile and Embarked are empty and cannot accept a truck on the tracked tank formation. The imported sprite is selected for every facing; rotation remains the renderer’s existing single-sprite responsibility. There are no Iraqi or other national MV registrations.

The equipment values append at 290–291, preserving all 290 earlier names/values and 256 template IDs. Save version remains11 and JsonPolicy is unchanged. Existing OOBs/scenes/PNG bytes/asset GUIDs/atlases are untouched. No stored unit is upgraded implicitly. Consumers must adopt the new catalog entries and deliberately author/re-export any new formations.

The 2S5 profile/census/USSR_SPA_2S5 template/SV_2S5 art were already connected and remain unchanged; a regression checks that chain. SV_AAA and SV_AN12 art were likewise already connected. The persisted TRN_AN8_SV key and three regional support profiles remain for the separately coordinated compatibility cleanup; this package does not rename them or remove their explicit missing-art exceptions.
